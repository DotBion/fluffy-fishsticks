(0.010863 baseline / 0.010263 sentiment, n_val=49) were produced with a
MinMaxScaler fitted on the whole file before the split, so the held-out
period's price range was known to the normalisation. The honest error is
higher. n_val is 40 rather than 49 because sequences are now cut inside
the training and validation slices separately instead of across the
boundary.

Two conclusions survive the rerun and both matter more than the delta:
  * sentiment's effect is smaller than the seed-to-seed spread, so on 251
    trading days of one ticker it is not a measurable improvement;
  * the model is beaten by predicting yesterday's close. That is the
    number to beat, and beating it is what the multi-ticker, multi-year
    panel exists to make possible.
